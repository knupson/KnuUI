## Description

**KnuUI** is an ElvUI plugin for **World of Warcraft: Forever** (1.60.1). It places your action bars,
unit frames, chat and data panels in a tidy block at the bottom centre of the screen, applies a dark
amber theme and ships its own logo, icons and AFK screen. English and Spanish.

### Requirements

- World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1
- ElvUI (the only required addon)

### Features

- **Guided installer** (`/knu`): apply everything at once or section by section.
- **Role profiles:** Tank, MDPS, RDPS and Healer. Healer moves party and raid to the centre and turns on
  incoming heals and HoT indicators; Tank adds threat borders; RDPS enlarges the cast bar and target debuffs.
  Each character remembers its role.
- **Column mode:** vertical health and mana columns next to the main bars.
- **Theme:** amber or neon, Quartz-style cast bar, OmniCC-style cooldown text, clean nameplates.
- **Extras:** combat glow on the screen edges, experience per hour and time to level, cinema mode.
- **Clean minimap:** one launcher button collects the minimap buttons.
- **Integrations:** Details! docking and skin, WoW-Pro, quest tracker, TomTom, Baganator, DynamicCam presets,
  and skins for Auctionator, BugSack, Leatrix Plus, Pawn, WhatsTraining, ThreatClassic2, GearQuestForever,
  Questie, GTFO and (opt-in) DBM.

### Notes

- Settings are written to your current ElvUI profile: copy the profile first if you want to keep it.
- Nothing is applied during combat.
- KnuUI only changes other addons' settings when you ask for it; see "What KnuUI changes in other addons"
  in the README.
- Source code and issues: https://github.com/knupson/KnuUI — MIT license.
