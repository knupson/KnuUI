## Description

The text below is the live description on CurseForge (Markdown editor).

KnuUI is an ElvUI plugin made for World of Warcraft: Forever. One installer gives you a compact amber layout, your own role profile and skins for the Forever addons listed below, with replacements for addons that no longer work there.

## Requirements

*   World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1
*   ElvUI (the only required addon)

## Why KnuUI on Forever

*   Quartz-style cast bar and OmniCC-style cooldown text are built in, and both work on Forever.
*   It is made for the Forever addon set: WoW-Pro (guide skin, auto accept and turn in fixed on non-English clients), Questie, TomTom, Shortest Path Forever, OpenMap, PallyPower, Pawn, Baganator, Details!, DBM and more.
*   When a guide points TomTom somewhere, the arrow follows Shortest Path Forever's route bend by bend.
*   With Offhand spanning two monitors, a command center puts guide, chat, Details, data bar, map and bags on the second screen and keeps only combat on the game screen.

## Features

*   The guided installer (`/knu`) applies each button right away and tells you what changed.
*   Role profiles for Tank, MDPS, RDPS and Healer, plus recommended UI builds per class and role.
*   17 colour palettes with a live preview, decoration, and a combat glow on the screen edges.
*   Column mode adds vertical health and mana columns next to the main bars, with compact side blocks.
*   A "+" button doubles the minimap and moves your buffs out of the way.
*   Export your whole interface as one text and import it on an alt or a friend's PC (`/knu export`, `/knu import`). Import always creates a new profile.
*   A flight timer that learns every route, a loot monitor for you and your group, and spell rank upgrades on your bars.
*   A raid marker bar with target and ground markers, ready check and pull timer.
*   One launcher button collects the minimap buttons of other addons; pin the ones you use most.
*   Skins for Auctionator, BugSack, DBM (opt-in), Details!, GatherMate2, GearQuestForever, GTFO, HandyNotes, Leatrix Plus, MaxDps, OpenMap, PallyPower, Pawn, Questie, RareScanner, Shortest Path Forever, ThreatClassic2, TomTom, WhatsTraining, WIM and WoW-Pro.

## Commands

*   `/knu` opens the installer · `/knu apply` applies everything · `/knu export` · `/knu import` · `/knu cinema`
*   `/knudiag` layout diagnostic

## Notes

*   Settings are written to your current ElvUI profile: export or copy it first if you want to keep it.
*   Nothing is applied during combat.
*   KnuUI only changes other addons' settings when you ask for it; see "What KnuUI changes in other addons" in the README.

## Other KNU addons

Made by the same author for Forever:

**[KnuCompanion](https://companion.knunet.com)**: a desktop app plus addon that puts your bags, bank, map and quests on your other monitor.

*   [KnuMail](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/knumail): simple, safe mailbox.
*   [KnuJunk](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/knujunk): marks and sells junk, repairs on its own.
*   [KnuCast](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/knucast): marks on your bars which spells work on the target.

All projects: [https://www.curseforge.com/members/knupwns/projects](https://www.curseforge.com/members/knupwns/projects)

## Descripción (castellano)

KnuUI es un plugin de ElvUI hecho para World of Warcraft: Forever. Un asistente (`/knu`) arma un diseño compacto ámbar, tu perfil de rol y skins para los addons de Forever, con reemplazos de addons que ya no andan (barra de lanzamiento estilo Quartz y texto de enfriamiento estilo OmniCC). Incluye 17 paletas, builds recomendadas por clase y rol, flecha de TomTom por la ruta más corta, centro de comando para dos monitores con Offhand, minimapa que se agranda con un botón y exportar o importar toda tu interfaz en un texto. Requiere sólo ElvUI. Si te gusta, marcalo como favorito (la estrella) y dejá un comentario. Si encontrás un error, contalo en los comentarios o en GitHub con el texto de BugSack y qué estabas haciendo. Otros addons de KNU para Forever: **[KnuCompanion](https://companion.knunet.com)**, una app de escritorio con su addon que lleva tus bolsas, banco, mapa y misiones a tu otro monitor. También [KnuMail](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/knumail) (buzón simple y seguro), [KnuJunk](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/knujunk) (marca y vende lo que no sirve, repara solo) y [KnuCast](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/knucast) (marca en las barras qué hechizos sirven contra el objetivo). Todos: [https://www.curseforge.com/members/knupwns/projects](https://www.curseforge.com/members/knupwns/projects).
